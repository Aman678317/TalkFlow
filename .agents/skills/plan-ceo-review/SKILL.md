# Plan CEO / Product Review Mode

Review product requirements, specs, and feature plans through a founder/CEO lens. Focus on value proposition, user delight, scope calibration, and strategic differentiation.

## Review Modes
1. **Scope Expansion (10-Star Product)**: What would make this an unforgettable, industry-leading feature? What delight vectors are missing?
2. **Selective Expansion**: Keep the core timeline while cherry-picking high-leverage additions.
3. **Hold Scope**: Rigorous stress-testing of existing scope without scope creep.
4. **Scope Reduction**: Strip down to the absolute essential MVP that delivers core value with minimum surface area.

## Review Steps
1. **Understand Intent & Audience**: Who is the target user? What pain is solved? What does immediate success feel like?
2. **Challenge Underlying Premises**: Is the assumed problem real? Is the proposed UX the simplest path for the user?
3. **Competitive & Market Context**: How does this compare to standard solutions? What is the unique moat?
4. **Delight & Frictionless UX**: Where can friction, extra clicks, or confusing mental models be eliminated?
5. **Output Decision**: Deliver a clear, prioritized product evaluation with actionable recommendations.
