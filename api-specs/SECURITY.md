# Security Policy

## Reporting Security Concerns & Vulnerabilities

We take the security and integrity of GlobalTalk AI, Desi Language AI, and our public specifications seriously.

If you believe you have discovered a vulnerability or security issue within our specifications or APIs, please notify our security team at:

**[security@globaltalk.ai](mailto:security@globaltalk.ai)**

Please include:
- A detailed summary of the vulnerability.
- Steps to reproduce or a proof of concept.
- Affected endpoints, parameters, or specifications.

We acknowledge receipt of vulnerability submissions within 48 hours and work with reporters on coordinated disclosure after remediation.
