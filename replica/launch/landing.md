## 2. The Problem (Addressing Real Customer Pain Points)
- **Pain Point 1: Invasive Bot Crashers**
  *Most multilingual meeting tools force an intrusive third-party recording bot into your call that distracts clients and records private audio.*
  **TalkFlow Fix**: Native in-browser WebRTC with zero virtual bots. Seamless audio tracks directly between participants.
- **Pain Point 2: Unbearable 4-Second Translation Lag**
  *Waiting 4 seconds after every sentence ruins spontaneous conversation and negotiation flow.*
  **TalkFlow Fix**: Sub-second concurrent streaming pipeline (VAD + chunked translation + neural TTS).

## 3. How It Works (The Core 3-Step Flow)
1. **Create a Room or Pick Your Languages**: Launch a video room in 10 seconds or open the dual-mic live translator.
2. **Share Your Link**: Send a clean link to your client or colleague. They join on their phone or desktop with zero app downloads or accounts.
3. **Speak & Be Heard**: Speak naturally in English, Spanish, Japanese, or 50+ languages. Listeners receive real-time translated audio and live bilingual captions.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Do my clients need to create an account or install software to join my meetings?**
A: No. Guests simply click your link, choose their preferred language, and enter the meeting directly in their mobile or desktop browser.

**Q: How does TalkFlow avoid having a bot in the meeting?**
A: TalkFlow is built natively on WebRTC. Audio translation occurs in real time within the media stream itself rather than through an external recording bot.

**Q: Can I cancel my subscription easily?**
A: Yes. You can cancel with a single click at any time from your billing dashboard through our Stripe customer portal.
