## 2. Color Palette & WCAG Compliance

- **Primary Interactive Brand**: `#0A66C2` (Enterprise Royal Blue)

- **Background & Surfaces**: `#FFFFFF` & `#F8F9FA` (Clean, modern contrast)
- **Accents**: Emerald Green `#15803d` (Live recording / speech success), Crimson `#b91c1c` (Destructive / alert).
- **Certified**: 100% WCAG 2.1 AA/AAA compliance verified with `contrast.py`.

---

## 3. Logo & Visual Asset Brief

- **Concept**: A sleek acoustic waveform subtly looping into an infinity ribbon or two speech bubbles seamlessly converging.
- **Mark Type**: Monogram / Icon + Wordmark in Inter typography.
- **Icon Specifications**:
  - Favicon: 16x16, 32x32 SVG crisp rendering.
  - Mobile App Icon: 1024x1024 PNG, solid background, zero alpha transparency.
  - Social / OpenGraph Image: 1200x630 PNG with high-contrast tagline.
- **Distinctiveness**: Strictly avoids the green speech bubble of WhatsApp, the camera icon of Zoom, or the globe motif of Google Translate.

---

## 4. Brand Voice & Tone

Three foundational pillars:
1. **Direct, not blunt**: Respect the user's intelligence; describe capabilities without buzzword inflation.
2. **Effortless, not casual**: Feel professional, reliable, and instantaneous.
3. **Inclusive, not patronizing**: Celebrate language diversity without exoticizing cultures.

### Do's and Don'ts

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| *"Speak naturally. Everyone hears their own language."* | *"Revolutionize your global AI paradigms."* |
| *"Send a link. Your guest joins in 1 click."* | *"Invite attendees to download the client app."* |
| *"Sub-second real-time voice translation."* | *"State-of-the-art super-fast AI bot."* |
| *"No credit card required. Cancel anytime."* | *"Contact sales for enterprise licensing tier."* |
| *"Export clean subtitles (SRT / VTT)."* | *"Download transcript recording."* |
